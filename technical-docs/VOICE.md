# VOICE.md: How we sound

## In one line
A knowledgeable production engineer talking to a buyer: specific, calm, and brief.

## Principles
1. **Numbers before adjectives.** "1.0 to 20 mm thick" beats "wide range of thicknesses".
2. **Say what it does.** "Seals transformer top covers" beats "high-quality solutions".
3. **One job per sentence.** Short sentences. Active voice. Plain verbs: make, cut, supply, ship.
4. **Claims need proof.** If we cannot show a test or a certificate, soften the claim or cut it (see `BRAND.md`).
5. **Respect the reader's time.** Lead with the answer. Buyers are often on a phone, on a shop floor.

## Language rules
- **Indian English, British spelling:** rubberised, colour, centre, programme.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. No ALL CAPS for emphasis.
- Use "we" for United Rubber Cork and "you" for the buyer.
- Write units with a space: 20 mm, not 20mm. Use "x" in sizes: 300 x 300 mm.
- Spell out a standard the first time: "IS 4253 Part II (Bureau of Indian Standards)", then "IS 4253 Part II". **[confirm the standard's full title]**
- No exclamation marks. No "world-class", "cutting-edge", "best-in-class", "solutions provider".
- Quote request, not "RFQ", in anything the buyer reads. Use "RFQ" only in internal docs and code.

## Tone by context
| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial product pages | Technical, compact | "Rubberised cork sheet, 1.0 to 20 mm thick. Cut to your drawing." |
| Application pages | Problem first, then fit | "Transformer top covers need a gasket that stays compressed and resists oil." **[confirm technical wording with United Rubber Cork]** |
| Decorative cork and gifts | Warmer, more sensory, still short | "Cork tiles with a natural grain. Quiet underfoot." |
| Forms and errors | Neutral, instructional, never apologetic | "Enter a phone number with at least 10 digits." |
| Sustainability | Factual, modest | "Cork bark is harvested without felling the tree." |

## Button and link vocabulary
Keep the same name for an action through the whole flow.

| Action | Label | Confirmation |
|---|---|---|
| Open the form | Request a quote | (page or dialog opens) |
| Send the form | Send quote request | "Quote request sent." |
| Chat | Send your drawing on WhatsApp | (WhatsApp opens) |
| Call | Call +91 99243 04617 | (dialer opens) |
| Download | Download datasheet (PDF, 240 KB) | (file downloads) |

Avoid: "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more", "Get a quote" on one page and "Enquire now" on another.

## Rewrites of the current copy
| Current | Rewrite | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "We have manufacturing Gasket as per customer's drawing or sample." | "We make gaskets to your drawing or sample." | Active voice, fixes grammar |
| "Rubberised Cork Gasket manufactured by us are completely leak proof as the same are made JOINTLESS." | "Our gaskets are made jointless, so there is no seam for fluid to find." | Explains the mechanism; drops the absolute claim. Restore "leak proof" only with test data |
| "ALight in weight, thermal resistant and impermeable to liquids/gases." | "Light, heat resistant, and impermeable to liquids and gases." | Fixes the typo, removes the slash |
| "ECO-FRIENDLY / EARTH NATURE / ENVIORNMENTALLY" | "Cork comes from bark. The tree keeps growing and is harvested again." | Replaces three labels with a fact |
| "If you only know cork as the stopper on your wine bottle, you are not alone." | "Most people know cork from wine bottles. It also seals transformer covers and vehicle gaskets, and insulates buildings." | Same warmth, adds the audience's real use cases |
| "Get a quote" (modal) | "Request a quote" | One name for one action |

## Microcopy
**Form labels**
Name · Company · Work email · Phone or WhatsApp · Product · Size and quantity (optional) · Drawing or sample photo (optional) · Message

**Helper text**
- Drawing upload: "PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB. DWG files: send by WhatsApp or email." **[confirm accepted file types]**
- Phone: "We may call to confirm the specification."

**Success**
"Quote request sent. We will reply to {email} within {time}." **[confirm reply time with United Rubber Cork; do not publish a promise they cannot keep]**

**Errors (say what is wrong and how to fix it)**
- Missing email: "Enter an email address so we can send the quote."
- Bad email: "That email looks incomplete. Check it and send again."
- File too large: "That file is over 10 MB. Compress it or send it by WhatsApp."
- Server problem: "The request did not send. Try again, or call +91 99243 04617."

**Empty or not found**
- 404: "This page has moved or does not exist. Go to Products, or request a quote."

## Sustainability wording
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "Renewable material" | "Eco-friendly" with no context |
| "Cork bark is harvested without felling the tree" | "Saves the planet" |
| "Pure cork lines are biodegradable" (once confirmed) | "All our products are decomposable" |
| "Natural cork base, bonded with rubber" for rubberised cork **[confirm composition wording]** | Hiding the rubber content |

## Words to avoid
leverage, solutions, synergy, state-of-the-art, premium quality, best price, customer satisfaction is our priority, one-stop, kindly, do the needful.

## Before publishing any text
- [ ] Every number has a source (datasheet, standard, certificate).
- [ ] No claim from the "Do not say" list in `BRAND.md`.
- [ ] Same term for the same thing as the navigation.
- [ ] Buttons say what will happen.
- [ ] Read aloud on a phone. If a paragraph runs past a screen, cut it or move the detail into a table.
