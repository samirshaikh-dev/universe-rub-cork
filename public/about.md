# About United Rubber Cork

United Rubber Cork supplies cork and rubberised cork products for industrial,
construction and insulation, decorative, and gifting applications. The
company is based in GIDC, Vapi, Gujarat, India.

## Products

The documented range includes rubberised cork sheets, gaskets, frames, strips
and washers; cork sheets and rolls, rings and gaskets, polishing wheels,
granules, anti-vibration slabs, Battricork and stoppers; decorative cork tiles
and memo boards; and cork gifts.

## Made to requirement

Rubberised cork gaskets and components can be discussed against a customer's
drawing or sample, or to IS 4253 Part II. Share the application, dimensions and
applicable specification to begin a product discussion.

## Quality information

Existing company information identifies ISO 9001:2015. Certificate ownership
