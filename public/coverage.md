# Applications and location

United Rubber Cork is based in GIDC, Vapi, Gujarat, India. The documented
audiences and application areas are transformer manufacturing, automotive
components, construction and insulation, and decorative applications.

This describes the company's location and documented application areas; it
does not establish shipping destinations or geographic service coverage.
Export capability and delivery coverage have not been confirmed.
