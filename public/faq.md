# United Rubber Cork — frequently asked questions

## What does United Rubber Cork make?

The documented range includes rubberised cork sheets, gaskets, frames, strips
and washers; cork sheets and rolls, rings and gaskets, polishing wheels,
granules, anti-vibration slabs, Battricork and stoppers; decorative cork tiles
and memo boards; and cork gifts. See [Products](/products).

## What product dimensions are published?

- Rubberised cork sheets: 1.0 to 20 mm thick; 2 ft x 3 ft, 3 ft x 3 ft and
  1000 x 1000 mm sizes.
- Rubberised cork strips: 20 to 600 mm wide and 1 to 10 mm thick.
- Cork granules: mesh sizes 0.2/0.5, 0.5/1, 1/2, 3/4 and 4/8 mm.
- Decorative cork tiles: 300 x 300 mm and 600 x 600 mm; 3 to 12 mm thick.

Confirm current availability and the full specification with the company.

## Can gaskets be made to a drawing or sample?

Rubberised cork gaskets and components can be discussed against a customer's
drawing or sample, or to IS 4253 Part II. Share the required dimensions and
application for a specification discussion.

## Is the ISO 9001:2015 certificate available?

Existing company information identifies ISO 9001:2015, but certificate
